Our story

Rooted in Lahore.
Cooked with fire.

Saffron House began with a simple idea: take the food we grew up with and cook it with the precision of a modern grill room. No shortcuts, no gas-fired substitutes — only charcoal, copper and patience.

Our kitchen sources from small farms across Punjab, hand-grinds its masalas every morning, and ages its beef in-house. The dining room is warm, low-lit and built for long conversations.
